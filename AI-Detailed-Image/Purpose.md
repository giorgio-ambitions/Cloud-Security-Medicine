This repository prototypes a physics‑informed AI engine for medical imaging — a technology designed to dramatically improve the precision and reliability of diagnostic interpretation.
Unlike conventional AI models that rely solely on data, our approach incorporates the physics behind MRI, CT, Ultrasound, and X‑ray acquisition, enabling superior generalization, robustness, and clinical trust.
This foundation supports future commercial applications in radiology automation, imaging‑as‑a‑service, and predictive diagnostics.
